Privacy Policy

Who we are

PHD Restaurant, 17 Apara Road, GRA, Port Harcourt 500102, Rivers State, is the data controller for personal data collected through this website, WhatsApp, phone and in the restaurant. This policy explains how we handle it under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as name, phone number and WhatsApp details.
  • Reservation, order and catering details, such as date, party size, delivery address and dietary or allergy needs.
  • Messages and feedback you send us.
  • Basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) processed by embedded services like Google Maps and Google Fonts.

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

How and why we use it

We use personal data to confirm and manage reservations and orders, answer enquiries, provide catering and delivery, keep our premises and service safe, and meet legal and accounting duties. Our lawful bases under the NDPA are: performance of a contract or steps you request before one; our legitimate interests in running and improving the restaurant; compliance with legal obligations; and your consent, for example for optional marketing messages, which you may withdraw at any time.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (such as WhatsApp, Google, payment and delivery partners) and with authorities where the law requires. Where a provider processes data outside Nigeria, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards as the NDPA requires.

Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal and accounting requirements, then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect it and will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and affected individuals of a qualifying breach as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your personal data, object to its processing (including direct marketing), and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with the NDPC (ndpc.gov.ng).

Children

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data without parental or guardian consent.
